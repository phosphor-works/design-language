Principles

One light

Surfaces are dark and still. Color is light, and light always means state: focus, selection or activity.

One body

A panel grows out of the control that opened it and returns to it. Nothing appears from nowhere.

Fast in, slow out

Motion starts at once and settles on a spring. Glow lags behind and fades, the way a phosphor does.

Name, value or action

Every string names a thing, shows a value or says what a control does. Anything else is removed.

Show the state

A preview, a fill level or a position replaces the sentence that would describe it.

Brand palette

#22D3EE #3B82F6 #A855F7 #F43F5E on #070F22
  • Blue is the default accent. A lit fill carries void ink, which reads at 5.6 to 1.
  • The spectrum appears once per screen, on the edge of the shell body, and in the marks.
  • Cyan marks the focused window, the keyboard focus ring and the terminal cursor.
  • Rose is reserved for errors, destructive actions and unread items.

Shape

20 − 6 padding = 14
  • An inner radius is the outer radius minus the padding between them.
  • Surfaces that touch fuse with a fillet. Surfaces that do not touch keep a gap of at least 10.
  • An active control becomes rounder. Tiles and the play button change shape with their state.

Motion

Grow

A surface opens from its source and closes back into it.

Stretch

The leading edge moves first and the trailing edge follows.

Squish

Controls compress under the pointer and release on a spring.

Afterglow

Light arrives at once and fades slowly.

Carrying it to Qt

SpringsPhosphorAnimation::Spring(omega, zeta) already exists. The three tokens become curve files next to phosphor-settle.
Fused bodyOne path per screen edge, drawn with QtQuick.Shapes or as a signed distance field in a surface shader. Panels become clipped content inside it instead of separate popups.
StretchTwo animated edges per indicator, one shared QML component for the bar, Facet's sidebar and Afterglow's tabs.
ColorThe roles in branding/palette/phosphor.toml replace the hand-tuned stops in the shell tokens and feed the app theme.
